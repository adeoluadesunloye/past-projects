About GrubKing

GrubKing is a cloud kitchen in Ogba, Lagos. We bring together partner kitchens such as Chicken Republic, Bukka Hot and Domino's so you can order from several menus in one place and get it delivered hot.

Our service

Order online from 9am to 10pm every day. Meals are cooked to order and delivered across Ikeja, Ogba, Maryland and Yaba.

Specials

  • Jollof Fridays: free drink with every jollof combo.
  • Family pack: four meals and a large drink at a reduced price.
  • Students: 10% off weekday lunches with a valid ID.

Homemade

Our homemade range covers Nigerian favourites like ofada rice, moi moi, pap and akara, and small chops, prepared fresh each morning.

Sponsors and partners

We work with local farms, drinks brands and delivery riders. To partner with us, email hello@grubking.ng.

Delivery

Delivery usually takes 30 to 45 minutes. A small fee applies by distance and is shown before you pay.

Order status

You get an SMS when your order is confirmed, when it leaves the kitchen and when the rider is close. For help with an order call +234 908 079 2345.

Payment options

Pay by card, bank transfer or cash on delivery.

FAQ

Can I order from two restaurants at once?
Yes. Add items from any partner kitchen to one cart.
Can I change my order?
Call us within five minutes of ordering and we will update it.
Do you cater for events?
Yes, contact us at least three days ahead.

Privacy policy

We use your name, phone number and address only to prepare and deliver your order and to send order updates. We do not sell your details. Ask us to delete them at any time.
