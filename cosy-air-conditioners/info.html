FAQ

Which size of air conditioner do I need?
As a guide, 1hp suits a room up to about 12 square metres, 1.5hp up to 18 and 2hp up to 25. Larger or sunnier rooms need more.
What is an inverter air conditioner?
An inverter unit adjusts its compressor speed instead of switching on and off, so it uses less power and keeps the room at a steadier temperature.
Do you install?
Yes. Standard installation is available in Lagos within three working days of delivery.
