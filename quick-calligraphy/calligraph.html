History Of Calligraphy

From the first time a quill hit the first piece of paper,humans have been looking for the most beautiful and decorative way to present their hand writing.
From holy texts to royal decrees, Chaucer to Shakespeare and Greek to Latin all writing was passed from generation to generation, strange to think about it in this technological age.It is said that the Romans were the first to brng calligraphy to the masses,you could look at the statues throughout Italy or Roman remains in the UK to see the strikingly beautiful lettering that was carved. Moreover, they also wrote in this style of writing.

What Is It All About?

What Is Calligraphy

Etymologically the word calligraphy finds its origin in the Greek word « kalos » meaning « beautiful ». At Graphiplus, we remain faithful to this spirit, for us, calligraphie is the art of beautiful writing. Calligraphy is a beautiful visual art related to writing.It is the execution and design of lettering with an ink brush,pen or other writing instruments.A modern calligraphic practice can be described as the art of giving forms to signs in a harmonious,expressive and experienced manner.

Modern Calligraphy

Modern calligraphy doesn’t necessarily meet the rules of traditionnal calligraphy. Through different times more styles and designs have been developed. It has been fashioned to look more modern and given way to create unique writing format that everyone loves. However, modern calligraphy is not totally ignorant of traditional rules. Indeed, for aesthetics reasons, it is often just a variant of an existing writing. For example, an « O » may be flattier or have a loop that goes lower than its traditional twin letter.
